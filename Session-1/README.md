# Session 1 - Architecture Journey, NFRs, and Trade-Offs

Session 1 introduces the overall training journey and sets the mindset for architecture decision-making.

## What This Session Achieved

- Introduced the purpose of the full Sonali Intellect training program.
- Explained why architecture is not about copying a popular pattern.
- Showed how NFRs and constraints shape technical decisions.
- Compared architecture styles through their trade-offs.
- Established the principle that the right architecture depends on the problem, team, risk, and operational context.

## Key Message

Architecture decisions should start from requirements, constraints, and business goals. Microservices, clean architecture, and other patterns are tools, not automatic answers.
